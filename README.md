## 📜 What Is This?

This isn't your average "list of projects and skills" website. It's a **crew of ten**, each member turned into their own living page — their own world, their own personality, their own little tricks up their sleeve. Think of it less like a resume, and more like flipping through the pages of a logbook washed up from the Grand Line.

No boring menus. No walls of text. Just **click into a character and explore.**

---

## ✨ Why It's Special

- 🎬 **Every page feels handmade** — no two look or behave alike, just like no two crewmates are alike.
- 🏴‍☠️ **A wanted-poster navigation drawer** lets you jump between crewmates anywhere on the site.
- 🌸 **Themed loading transitions** — every character has their own signature animation and sound when you sail into their page.
- 🎮 **Hidden interactions everywhere** — games, sound, secrets, and easter eggs tucked into almost every page.
- ⚔️ **No two visits are the same** — it rewards curiosity. Click around. Scroll slowly. Look for what's hidden.

---

## 🧭 How to Explore

1. Open **`index.html`** — that's the dock where the journey begins.
2. Click **Set Sail** to meet Luffy first, or pick any crewmate portrait to jump straight to their page.
3. Use the wanted-poster drawer (📌 icon) on any page to hop to another crew member.
4. Scroll, click, and poke around — most of the magic isn't obvious at first glance.
