Log Entry · 2013

First Contact

Grade 4. A cousin's PC during a countryside visit. That was it — the moment the curiosity locked on and never really let go.

Log Entry · 2015

The Hard Drive Incident

Grade 6, right after a lesson on how hard drives worked. Took the home computer's drive apart to see for himself. Broke the read/write arm in the process. No regrets — every sniper starts somewhere.

Log Entry · 2019

Down the Rabbit Hole

Grade 9. Found the "A2D Channel" on YouTube, which led to PC Master Race, which led to Linus Tech Tips, which led to… well, most of what's built into this website.

Log Entry · 2025–2026

Two Cores and a Voucher Shock

Set out to earn CompTIA A+ without realizing the exam had two separate cores. Sat Core 1 in June 2025, Core 2 that September. Also nearly fainted at the price of the exam voucher.

Log Entry · March 2026

Network+, Locked In

Rehired as a Security Analyst at Insightz Technology — earned the CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) shortly after, since it would actually help with the job.

Log Entry · 2026

The Year of Linux Gaming

Declared it himself. Switched primary OS to Bazzite after it ran Forza Horizon 6 better than Windows did. Somewhere around the same time, this One Piece obsession turned into a themed rEFInd bootloader — and eventually, this entire website.
